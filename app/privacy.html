Webdelo Content Platform

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Internal tool. The Webdelo Content Platform is an internal web application accessible only to authorized Webdelo team members. It is not a public service.

Who the operator is

The Webdelo Content Platform is operated by Webdelo (webdelo.com). The application is the company's internal system for keyword research, semantic core building, and content planning. Only the operator's authorized staff have access to its interface.

How access works

Access to the application requires authentication with personal credentials. Accounts are created and managed manually by the Webdelo administrator. There is no public registration, no self-service sign-up, and no guest access. Third parties cannot independently create an account or gain entry to the system.

What data the application receives

Google Ads API data

When an authorized team member uses the platform, the application may retrieve the following through the Google Ads API:

  • Keyword ideas and suggestions
  • Historical search volume data for keywords
  • Search query frequency trends
  • Competition and bid-related metrics (where available through the API)
  • Keyword forecasting data

The application accesses Google Ads data exclusively in read-only mode. It does not create, modify, or manage campaigns, ads, or any other entities in the connected Google Ads account.

User account data

  • Internal login credentials (provisioned by the administrator).
  • Session identifiers for the duration of active use.
  • Usage logs: timestamps of API requests, feature usage, and technical identifiers for troubleshooting.

Research data

  • Keyword lists and semantic cores created by users.
  • Saved search queries and analysis results.
  • Project and workspace metadata.

What the data is used for

All data received and generated within the platform is used exclusively for internal Webdelo business purposes:

  • Keyword research and discovery for client projects.
  • Building and refining semantic cores.
  • Search-demand analysis and content opportunity identification.
  • Feeding keyword data into Webdelo's internal content production pipeline.
  • Tracking research history and comparing keyword performance over time.

Usage logs and technical identifiers are used for request logging, rate limiting, and troubleshooting.

Where the data is stored

Data is stored on the operator's servers that host the Webdelo Content Platform. Keyword research data and semantic cores are retained in project workspaces accessible to authorized team members. Session and usage logs are stored for operational purposes.

Who has access

Only authorized staff of Webdelo, within their assigned roles, can access the application and its data. Access permissions are managed internally by the administrator. The data is not shared with third parties, clients, or external contractors outside the scope of authorized Webdelo operations.

Data is not sold

Webdelo does not sell Google user data. Data obtained through Google APIs is used exclusively by authorized Webdelo team members for internal analysis and content planning. It is not sold, licensed, shared for advertising purposes, or made available to the public. The platform does not use Google data to build third-party advertising profiles or to inform advertising targeting for external parties.

Retention period

Keyword research data and semantic cores are retained for the duration of active projects plus 90 days after project closure. Usage logs with technical identifiers are kept for a limited period needed to investigate errors and prevent abuse. Users may delete their research data at any time through the application interface.

How to request deletion

Authorized users can delete their keyword research data and semantic cores directly within the application. To request deletion of account data or access revocation, contact the Webdelo administrator at webdelo.stream@gmail.com. Deletion requests are fulfilled within 14 business days.

Security

The application enforces authentication for all access. Data in transit is encrypted via HTTPS. Access to the hosting infrastructure is restricted to authorized Webdelo technical staff. The Google Ads API integration uses OAuth 2.0 with the minimum required scopes limited to read access.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the application evolves. Changes will be communicated internally to all authorized users and reflected on this page.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, data handling practices, or to exercise your data rights, contact the Webdelo administrator at webdelo.stream@gmail.com.

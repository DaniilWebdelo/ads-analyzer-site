ShareSpider

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

ShareSpider is an internal tool. It is not publicly distributed and access is granted manually by the administrator of Webdelo Studio.

This Privacy Policy describes how ShareSpider ("the Application", "we", "our") — a macOS-native desktop application developed and used exclusively by the internal team of Webdelo Studio — handles data.

Google Ads data is used solely to provide campaign analysis and recommendations. ShareSpider does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising purposes.

1. Scope & Applicability

ShareSpider is a closed internal software tool. It is not sold, licensed, or distributed to the general public. Access is provisioned manually by the Webdelo administrator and protected by a mandatory internal authentication form.

This policy applies to all authorized users within the Webdelo team who operate the Application.

2. Data Access Model: Read-Only

ShareSpider connects to third-party advertising platforms — primarily Google Ads — exclusively in read-only mode. The Application:

  • Retrieves Search Terms, ads, keywords, and campaign performance metrics for analysis.
  • Never modifies, creates, pauses, or deletes campaigns, ads, keywords, or any other entity.
  • Never changes bids, budgets, or targeting settings.
  • Does not perform automatic optimizations or automated rule-based actions.

All decisions based on collected data are made by human PPC specialists — the Application provides information, not autonomous action.

3. Information Collected

3.1 Campaign Data (via Google Ads API)

  • Search term reports
  • Keyword performance data
  • Ad copy and creative metadata
  • Impression share, click-through rates, conversion metrics
  • Lost impression share and lost budget estimates
  • Landing page URLs associated with campaigns

This data is retrieved through OAuth 2.0 with the minimum required scopes for read access.

3.2 Landing Page Content

ShareSpider fetches and analyzes the content of landing pages linked to ad campaigns for relevance auditing. No personal data of website visitors is collected.

3.3 User Authentication Data

  • Internal login credentials (managed and provisioned by the Webdelo administrator).
  • Session tokens for the duration of active use.

4. How Data Is Used

All collected data is used exclusively for internal analysis by the Webdelo team:

  • Audit landing page relevance against campaign keywords.
  • Identify underperforming ads and wasted budget.
  • Generate unified performance reports for PPC specialists.
  • Track campaign quality metrics over time.
  • Correlate lead volume drops with market-wide search trends.

5. Data Storage & Security

  • Campaign data is stored locally on the user's macOS device and optionally in a shared workspace accessible to authorized Webdelo team members.
  • All local storage is protected by macOS file-system encryption (FileVault, where enabled).
  • OAuth tokens are stored in the macOS Keychain.
  • No campaign data is transmitted to external servers outside of Google's own APIs.

6. Data Sharing & Third Parties

ShareSpider does not share, sell, rent, or trade campaign data with any third party. Data remains within:

  • The local device of the authorized Webdelo specialist.
  • Internal Webdelo infrastructure used for team collaboration.
  • Google's infrastructure — solely via the official Google Ads API, under Google's own data processing terms.

No analytics, telemetry, or tracking SDKs are embedded in the Application. No usage data is sent to ShareSpider developers or Webdelo administrators beyond what is visible in shared workspace files.

7. Data Retention

Campaign data and audit reports are retained for the duration of active analysis projects plus 90 days after project closure. After this period, data is automatically purged from shared workspaces.

OAuth tokens can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page.

8. How to Request Data Deletion

Authorized users can delete their data at any time:

  • Local data: Delete the Application's workspace folder from your macOS device — or use the built-in data management option in Settings.
  • Shared workspace data: Contact the Webdelo administrator at webdelo.stream@gmail.com. Deletion requests are fulfilled within 14 business days.
  • Google Ads data: Revoke the Application's OAuth access from your Google Account. The Application will no longer be able to retrieve new data — and existing local data can be deleted as above.

9. Read-Only Guarantee

ShareSpider is architecturally incapable of modifying your Google Ads account. The OAuth scopes requested are explicitly limited to read-only access. No code path exists in the Application that could create, update, pause, or delete campaigns, ads, keywords, bids, or budgets. This is a fixed design constraint — not a configurable option.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the Application evolves. Changes will be communicated internally to all authorized users and reflected on this page.

11. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, data handling practices, or to exercise your data rights, contact the Webdelo administrator at webdelo.stream@gmail.com.

How ShareSpider works

🔒

Read-Only Data Collection

Connects to Google Ads via read-only access — fetches Search Terms, ads, and keywords. No modifications, no automatic changes. Human decisions only.

🎯

Relevance Detection

Audits landing pages against campaign keywords. Flags irrelevant content and mismatched messaging that wastes ad spend.

🧠

AI-Powered Analysis

Processes data arrays with formulas and heuristics, then builds a structured prompt for AI evaluation — surfacing actionable recommendations.

📊

Performance History

Stores historical Search Terms, traffic structure, CPA, CVR, and conversion clusters. Compare periods and surface trends — not just losses.

⚡

Batch Automation

Run audits across 2–3 accounts simultaneously. Unified reporting merges findings into a single actionable document.

🌐

Full-Site Crawl

For campaigns with many landing pages: crawl the entire site. Settings let you configure depth, exclusions, and schedules.

🛡️

Read-only by design

ShareSpider never writes to your ad accounts. All data is collected for analysis only. Recommendations are delivered to specialists — the human makes the final call.
